# Content Bible — Ocean Wave VIP

## Positioning
**Category:** Private celebration charters on Vancouver's waterfront.
**Core promise to test:** “Make waves. Make memories.” Invite a trusted group to celebrate a special occasion aboard a private yacht with personalized planning.
**Emotional outcome:** an intimate, uncommon, shareable celebration.
**Functional outcome:** choose suitable vessel, understand cost, check date, request quote or consult.

## Editorial pillars
1. **Occasion inspiration (35%)** — birthdays, graduation, retirement, bachelorette/bachelor, bridal, friends and fresh starts.
2. **Vessel reality & trust (25%)** — verified walk-throughs, captain/crew policies, capacity, pickup, inclusions, weather and cancellation.
3. **Planning utility (25%)** — what does a 4-hour yacht party cost, guests/capacity, logistics, FAQs, what happens after quote.
4. **Authentic proof & partners (15%)** — permitted testimonials, event photographer collaborations and behind-the-scenes readiness.

Mix is a creative planning assumption, not performance evidence.

## Voice
Contemporary, welcoming, polished, celebratory and clear. Avoid overstated wealth/luxury, rowdy or unsafe partying, personal-status targeting, claims about weather guarantees, “unlimited drinks,” or exaggerated scarcity. Use “private yacht celebration” and “check your date”; use “complimentary consultation” only if client approves a free human consultation offering.

## P.S.S.O. templates
- **Problem:** “Planning a [occasion] for [group] that feels different from a restaurant?”
- **Stats:** only actual cited keyword/consumer evidence or first-party conversion metrics; never “X% of Vancouver buyers” without a sample.
- **Solution:** private yacht + verified vessel capacity, transparent logistics, guided quote process.
- **Offer:** “Check date and get a quote” or “Schedule a 15-minute planning call.”

## Funnel copy blocks (proposed)
**Hero:** “Celebrate [Occasion] on a Private Vancouver Yacht.” Subhead: “Bring your favourite people together for a memorable experience on the waterfront. Tell us your date and group size to explore suitable options.” CTA: **Check Availability & Get a Quote**. Secondary: **Plan My Celebration**.

**Trust module:** “See the actual yacht, understand what your charter includes, and get a personalized quote before committing.” Only show actual, approved assets.

**FAQ prompts:**
- How many people can we bring?
- What does a 4-hour charter include and cost?
- Is a captain included?
- What happens in bad weather?
- Can we bring catering, cake or decorations?
- What is the pickup/boarding point?
- How far in advance should we inquire?
- What confirms the reservation?

Leave answers blank until owner policy verification; don't publish placeholders as definitive.

## 4-week editorial plan (draft)
| Week | Reels / short video | Static carousel | FAQ / landing content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real vessel tour / group size | “Birthday vs restaurant venue” | Prices/inclusions explained |
| 2 | Bachelorette / stagette scene | 5 questions to ask charter operators | Date/guest-count booking explainer |
| 3 | Graduation / retirement story | Planning checklist for 10–15 guests | Weather, cancellations |
| 4 | Milestone / friend-group night | Verified social proof or vessel tour | Compare charter options |

Publish only with media and operator approval. If filming is not available, create clearly labeled concepts, not fabricated real-event photos.

## Creative QA
- [ ] Correct vessel/fleet capacity for the displayed boat
- [ ] Actual or licensed assets; content rights and participant permission
- [ ] No fake reviews, ratings, award badges or “best” claims
- [ ] Pricing fully qualified including minimums, fees and taxes
- [ ] No sensitive personal-status targeting or assumed viewer divorce
- [ ] Mobile safe zones, readable captions, accurate CTA, no broken link
- [ ] Owner reviewed the approved version and landing destination
