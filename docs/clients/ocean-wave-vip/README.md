## Business objective
Generate qualified event inquiries and celebration-planning appointments; convert them into *paid, confirmed, profitable* private charters. Two pathways: **Check charter availability and get a quote** for high-intent visitors, and **Book a 15-minute celebration consultation** for planners. Consultation scheduling is not vessel inventory confirmation.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Owner-verified fact and approved contract/operating policy.
2. Current official Ocean Wave site and permissioned client system data (dated).
3. Public competitor/consumer material with working source link and observation date.
4. Analyst inference, campaign hypothesis and scenario assumptions.

**Known conflict:** the initial client briefing describes three vessels; the public-site audit referenced two named vessels. Do not advertise three available vessels or claim specific capacities until owner verifies live availability. Some previous research mentions Sunny Great (up to 12, starting C$400/h) and Meridian 441 (up to 15, starting C$500/h); these are historical site observations requiring a new check.
