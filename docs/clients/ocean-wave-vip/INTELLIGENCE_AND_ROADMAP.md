# Intelligence, competitors, social listening & 30-day launch

## Intelligence questions
- Which Vancouver operators offer party charters for a similar group size?
- Which operators publish total pricing, inclusions and weather terms?
- What recurring customer questions indicate demand for small-group celebration venues?
- Which charter experiences are explicitly requested but underserved?
- Which occasions produce actual profitable inquiries and bookings for Ocean Wave?

## Candidate competitor watchlist (verify before claims)
- [SeaSky Boat Rental / yacht offerings](https://www.seaskyboatrental.com/)
- [Vancouver Yachts](https://book.vancouveryachts.ca/)
- [Dream Yacht Charters](https://www.dreamyacht.ca/)
- [Yoho Cruises](https://www.yohocruises.com/)
Keep operators, intermediaries and larger party boats in distinct comparison categories; do not claim like-for-like pricing.

For every verified offer: operator name, source URL, inspected_at, boat/capacity, charter minimum, base price currency/unit, inclusions, policy, occasion packages, booking UX and confidence. Normalize *total effective comparable charter cost*, never raw advertised hourly rate alone.

## Social listener design (not deployed)
Sources: permitted public review pages and social discussions, authorized client reviews, inbound event forms, post-call lost-reason summaries. Exclude private groups, personal details and restricted scraped sources. Record platform, source URL, post date, observation date, short excerpt, customer need, frequency (unique items), event type, purchase intent, commercial fit and confidence. Not representative market research.

Core themes: birthday ideas, group of 10–15, price transparency, weather, coverage, travel to boarding location, catering/decorations, trust and reputation, quick response, graduation season and partner-led bookings.

## Opportunity miner
Scores 0–5 each: unique-signal frequency 25%, commercial intent 25%, client fit 20%, contribution potential 15%, competitor gap 10%, evidence quality 5%. Weighted score out of 100 = 20 × weighted-average. Confidence separately: low/medium/high. Flag operator constraints before recommending packages.

**Hypotheses:** transparent all-in quotes; birthday for intimate groups; stagette experience; seasonal graduation; corporate/family retirement; cross-vessel date alternatives. All unvalidated until first-party conversion and owner feedback.

## 30-day action plan
| Phase | Owner | Deliverable / acceptance |

| Days 14–21 | Operator + account lead | Monitor qualified inquiries and appointments, learn objections, adjust creative |
| Days 21–30 | Growth lead + finance | Review actual bookings, contribution margin and acquisition economics; pause/scale decision |

## KPI model
- Prospect traffic, landing conversion, qualified inquiries, contact SLA, appointment scheduling, show rate, quote conversion, deposit-paid charters, gross booked CAD, contribution after acquisition spend, fleet occupancy.
- Illustrative trial budget: C$1,500/month; target 4–6 bookings at hypothetical C$1,800 average charter (C$7,200–C$10,800 gross) — **not a forecast**.
- Unit economics: actual paid charters × realized contribution per charter − incremental ad spend − incremental delivery/service costs. Validate margins with operator.

## Human approval gates
H0 verify client identity/fleet/policies; H1 source/legal review; H2 approve creative claims/assets; H3 approve funnel/webhook/privacy; H4 authorize ad spend/publication; H5 quote and deposit decisions. Draft documents do not authorize ads, customer contact or production release.
