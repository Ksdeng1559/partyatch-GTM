## 3. LeadOS 14-section operating framework

### 1 — System overview
**Community discovery → organizer suitability → permissioned collaboration/content → occasion landing page → quote or consultation → vessel-fit verification → owner-issued quote → deposit/confirmed charter.**

### 2 — Problem
Organizers compare many venues, wonder about group-size fit, price, policies and responsiveness. A group outing may not be explicitly searching for a yacht.

### 3 — Emotional core
Celebrating meaningful occasions with close friends, colleagues and family; privacy, memorable photography and low planning friction.

### 4 — Root cause
Yacht charter is often categorized as rental rather than celebration venue; offers may require a phone call before a buyer understands price and capacity.

### 5 — Viral-to-Lead
| Journey | Format | Hook | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Real vessel Reel | “Your birthday deserves more than another restaurant” | Visit celebrations page |
| Consideration | Organizer-friendly checklist | “What to confirm for a private waterfront party” | Compare vessel and policy details |
| Conversion | Event-specific offer | “Check your celebration date” | Quote request / planning call |

### 6 — Promise
**“Explore a private Vancouver yacht celebration and get a personalized quote.”** Not a promise of yacht availability until confirmed by operator.

### 7 — Platform matrix
| Channel | Role | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook/Instagram | Paid/organic celebration discovery | Qualified inquiries |
| Meetup/community organizers | Permissioned private-event collaboration | Partner introductions, referred bookings |
| Wedding vendors | Referral engine | Referred inquiry → booked charter |
| Google Search / Local SEO | Capture explicit charter/party rental demand | Quote requests / bookings |
| Email to interested partners | Human-reviewed, legally permitted B2B approach | Approved meetings |

### 8 — CTA + DM strategy
Primary: **Check Availability & Request a Quote**. Secondary: **Book a 15-Minute Planning Consultation**. A group moderator's permission or public listing is not consent to send bulk DMs. Use platform-native allowed links/forms and follow-ups only after a legitimate inquiry, permission, and appropriate messaging consent. No cold auto-DMs.

### 9 — Ad + SEO synergy
Pair birthday and bachelor/bachelorette Reels with relevant occasion pages, and core “yacht rental Vancouver” search traffic with the quote-first charter page. Treat social engagement and organic search volumes as different metrics.

### 10 — AI + automation
LeadSniperAI/Harvey may *research and verify* organizers; Hermes orchestrates evidence and human checkpoints; Supabase/CommercialKonnect logs account/event source; Cal.com schedules *consultations only*; Resend/Twilio send authorized transactional messages. No integration is claimed live.

### 11 — Optional educational content
Produce an organizer checklist: group size, vessel match, all-in pricing, boarding, weather, catering/decoration approval and deposit terms.

### 12 — Implementation blueprint
| Phase | Action | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | Validate 15 relevant communities/partner candidates | URL, type, recent activity and rules recorded |
| Qualify | Identify up to 10 actual organizers/decision-makers | Evidence of authority, contact route and fit |
| Propose | Prepare five tailored collaboration proposals | All claims, vessel details and offer conditions reviewed |
| Engage | Human-approved outreach where legally permitted | Permission and response logged |
| Convert | Route referred interested visitors to event pages | Source/UTM tracked; quote or consult received |
| Learn | Review actual deposit-paid charters | Real contribution after acquisition costs |

### 13 — Guiding principles
No fabricated membership totals, moderator approvals, customer consent or conversion claims. Do not enter groups to advertise contrary to posted rules. No scraping private membership directories or inferred sensitive life circumstances. Follow CASL, privacy rules and all platform/community terms.

### 14 — Next steps
Verify the links, conduct community fit and moderator checks, choose three high-confidence organizer targets, draft customized partnership approaches and seek human approval before any outreach or expenditure.
