## Creative test design
- First 14-day spend proposal: C$30–50/day *total*, subject to owner approval; run 2–3 creatives rather than 12 underfunded ad sets. No launch/spend authorized.
- Optimize first for qualified inquiry and later for confirmed charters once event volume supports it.
- Test three visual framings: real vessel walkthrough, celebration montage, scenic intimate-group experience. Require media rights.
- Scripts: 0–3s occasion hook, 3–8s real yacht + Vancouver, 8–12s proof/clear group fit, 12–15s single CTA.
- Measure creative/campaign → landing page → inquiry → consultation/quote → booked charter.

## Targeting & compliance
Geography: Vancouver/Greater Vancouver with travel catchment tested from qualified inquiry data. Use broad age-appropriate prospecting, video engagement where allowed and compliant retargeting after valid consent and audience thresholds. Do not use protected/sensitive personal-life inference or relationship-status custom audiences. Avoid ad copy such as “Recently divorced?” that asserts personal attributes. Event activities (alcohol, swimming, music, late-night cruises) are not promised unless operator policies explicitly allow them.

## Partner channel
Wedding planners, photographers, event decorators, concierges, hotels, corporate event coordinators. LeadSniperAI/Harvey research → BANT fit → human-reviewed Sales Bird/Mystrika outreach only with appropriate legal basis → partnership meeting → documented referral terms. No scraped DM spam or unauthorized SMS.

## 30-day allocation hypothesis
Week 1 setup and owner validation; week 2 launch birthdays + bachelorette; week 3 compare quality and objections, add retirement/graduation only if seasonally viable; week 4 allocate to campaigns with actual contribution-positive bookings. Do not describe aspirational appointment counts as historic performance.
