### What these ranks mean strategically
1. **Broad charter terms are the proven competitor footprint:** SeaSky occupies top-10 positions for relevant boat/yacht rental terms, particularly ranks 5–8. The opportunity is to create an evidence-rich, differentiated private-event page; **no Ocean Wave gap can be quantified without its own position data**.
2. **High volume does not equal high fit:** “harbour cruise,” “pontoon,” “kayak,” “boat tour,” and “Granville Island boat rentals” may reflect sightseeing, self-drive, boat types or boarding locations that Ocean Wave does not offer. Do not spend or make landing pages for them without service-fit verification.
3. **Demand concentration is imperfectly described by exporter:** Repeated 1,900-volume variants likely overlap. Don't sum them to estimate unique shoppers.
4. **Event landing-page strategy is the differentiation hypothesis:** Compete on complete birthday, stagette and intimate celebration solutions rather than copying generic boating pages.

## D. Keyword-to-funnel plan
**Search-driven:** Exact/phrase “yacht rental vancouver,” “boat rental vancouver,” “yacht charter vancouver bc,” “boat party rental vancouver,” “rent a yacht vancouver”; segment conversion with **Check availability & request quote**. Use budget caps and query-negative review. Verify legal category and services first.

**Social-driven:** Birthday/milestone, bachelor/bachelorette, retirement and graduation concepts use authentic yacht imagery with occasion-specific messages. Send to occasion-selecting page with two paths: quote request **or** 15-minute planning consultation. Meta cannot be expected to inherit Google's CPC or search volumes.

**SEO-driven:** One verified charter category page; celebration hub; unique birthday page; stagette/bachelor pages when differentiated; graduation/retirement seasonal pages if validated. Existing Ocean Wave indexed URLs must be audited to prevent cannibalization.

## E. Competitor gap experiments
1. **Transparent pricing / minimums:** test a FAQ comparing four-hour sample package cost (only after owner provides exact inclusions and approved wording).
2. **12–15 guest experience:** use actual vessel-specific capacity and verified photos; no blanket group-size promise.
3. **Occasion-first messaging:** A/B “Your Birthday Deserves More Than Another Restaurant” vs “Private Celebration on Vancouver's Waterfront.”
4. **Fast response:** measure inquiry-to-human-contact time, aiming for <5 minutes during supported hours.
5. **Lead quality vs traffic:** track qualified inquiries, quote issuance, deposit-paid charters, contribution margin; don't optimize purely for volume.
