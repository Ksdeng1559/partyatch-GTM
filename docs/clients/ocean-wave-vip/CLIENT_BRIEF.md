## Buyer and occasion segments
**P1 evergreen:** birthdays, milestone birthdays (30/40/50/60), bachelorette/stagette and bachelor/stag celebrations.
**P1 conditional/seasonal:** graduation (spring/early summer), retirement (corporate and family).
**P2 test:** bridal showers, girls'/guys' nights, anniversaries, engagements, family celebrations and positive new-beginnings/divorce celebration concepts.
**B2B referral:** wedding and event planners, photographers, decorators, hotels/concierges, corporate HR/event coordinators.

The buyer often organizes the event for someone else (friend, spouse, parent, colleague); target the *planner need* rather than inferring the viewer's personal status.

## Customer jobs
- Find an exclusive small-group celebration venue.
- Understand the total price and permitted group size before committing.
- Know what is included, how weather changes plans and whether the operator is trustworthy.
- Get a fast response about the preferred date.
- See authentic vessel-specific photos and decide without complex scheduling.
- Arrange accessible optional amenities and logistics with a real coordinator.

## Differentiation hypothesis
“A private celebration venue on Vancouver's waterfront” rather than undifferentiated hourly boat rental. Core messaging emphasizes privacy, group experience, real boat proof, transparent planning and booking convenience. Do not assert *best*, luxury credentials, guaranteed views, or operator policies without evidence.
